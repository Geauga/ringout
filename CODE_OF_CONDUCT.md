# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct`n`n## Our Pledge`n`nWe as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.`n`nWe pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.
